What is 3weirdaxes?

3weirdaxes is a political quiz that measures your values across three primary metrics: economic (left vs. right), cultural (progressive vs. conservative), and worldview (idealist vs. materialist). You will be presented with a series of statements and asked to express your opinion, from Strongly Agree to Strongly Disagree. Each answer will influence your final scores. Answer as honestly as possible!

There are questions in the test.

What are the axes?

The quiz evaluates you across three distinct axes. Each axis represents a spectrum between two opposing viewpoints:

EQUALITY

Those with higher Equality scores believe the economy should distribute value evenly among the populace. They tend to support progressive tax codes, social programs, and at high values, socialism.

ECONOMIC

MARKETS

Those with higher Market scores believe the economy should be focused on rapid growth. They tend to support lower taxes, privatization, deregulation, and at high values, laissez-faire capitalism.

TRADITION

Those with higher Tradition scores believe in traditional values and strict adherence to a moral code. They are usually religious, and support the status quo or the status quo ante.

CULTURAL

PROGRESS

Those with higher Progress scores believe in social change and rationality. They are usually secular or atheist, and support environmental action and scientific or technological research.

IDEALISM

Those with higher Idealism scores believe that ideas, values, and abstract concepts are the primary drivers of history and human progress. They focus on philosophy, ethics, and moral imperatives.

WORLDVIEW

MATERIALISM

Those with higher Materialism scores believe that physical, economic, and tangible conditions are the primary drivers of history. They prioritize systemic changes, material security, and practical outcomes.


What's the "Closest Match" mean at the bottom of the results?

In addition to matching you to the eight values, the quiz also attempts to match you to a political ideology. This is a work in progress and is much less accurate than the values and axes, so don't take it too seriously. If you disagree with your assigned ideology, send us an email at 3weirdaxes@tuta.com with your scores, matched ideology, and preferred ideology, and we'll look into adjusting the system. Thanks!
